- `get_data` says in `meta.notes` what a count of 0 may mean: a split names the years it
  passed over for counting no record, and an answer of one `year` without records says
  that Quick Stats counted none, the count it also gives a year not yet published, since
  Quick Stats names no release. An answer with records carries no such note.
